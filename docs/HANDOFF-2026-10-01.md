## 3. Lessons

- **Check existing reports for overlap before scoping.** `agent-harnesses` already existed. The new report
  was scoped as methods rather than approaches, so the two complement each other instead of duplicating.
  About 10 repos appear in both, under different lenses.
- **Secondary surveys are convenient but compound error.** Much of the 2026 harness evidence reaches you
  through marmelab's survey. Mark second-hand figures explicitly in the generator rather than smoothing
  them into prose.
- **Several highly relevant repos are not starred**, so they are not in the report: `gmickel/flow-next`
  (the only repo marmelab found with before/after harness measurements), `ai-boost/awesome-harness-engineering`,
  `humanlayer/12-factor-agents`, `laude-institute/terminal-bench`, `UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_ai` and
  `SWE-agent/mini-swe-agent`. See §4.
